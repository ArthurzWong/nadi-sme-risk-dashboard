# Brand — Nadi SME Intelligence

_Status: user-specified_

## Direction

Professional Malaysian fintech dashboard. Use a dark graphite interface, clear sans-serif typography, and plain-language copy. Show monetary values in RM and keep the Malaysia context visible.

## Palette

- Background: `#0A0E0C`
- Panels: `#101613` and `#151D18`
- Primary text: `#E8EEE9`
- Secondary text: `#AAB5AD`
- Green status and primary action: `#A6E7B0` / `#82D994`
- Amber warning: `#EDBD68`
- Red high-risk state: `#FF8581`

Use green, amber, and red only for their semantic status meanings. Keep supporting borders and surfaces in cool, low-contrast graphite tones while preserving readable text contrast.

## Typography and voice

- Sans-serif interface text; monospaced figures and metadata.
- Plain, concise, active language for SME owners.
- Label estimates as illustrative until policy-specific model logic is supplied.
